07 / QUESTIONS

Know what you're
connecting.

Is this the same thing as Desktop Commander?+

No. ShanX13 AiGG Direct Commander is an independently branded product under development. The local MCP engine may use properly attributed MIT-licensed open-source components, but Desktop Commander's commercial cloud service and design are separate proprietary products.

Can customers control the owner's computers?+

No. Every customer must authenticate and enroll their own approved device with isolated permissions. A public listing does not grant access to anyone else's system.

Are usage figures generated or simulated?+

Production usage will come from authenticated server records. The interface illustrations on this page are concepts and do not represent live machine activity.

Can I purchase a plan today?+

No. Pricing, checkout, public plugin approval, and account provisioning are not yet live. This page does not collect payments.
